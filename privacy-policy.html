Privacy Policy — Basketball Fever AI Clipper

Last updated: October 18, 2026

Basketball Fever AI Clipper helps users create basketball highlight clips and prepare videos for social media publishing.

Information We Access

If you connect your TikTok account, the application may receive authorization information and access tokens needed to perform video-posting actions you approve.

How Information Is Used

Information is used to authenticate your account, facilitate uploads you request, and maintain the application's functionality.

Storage and Security

Authorization tokens are intended to be stored locally by the uploader. Keep credentials and configuration files private.

Sharing

We do not sell personal information. Videos are transmitted to TikTok when you authorize an upload and are processed under TikTok's policies.

Data Retention and Deletion

You can revoke TikTok authorization and delete locally stored authorization files to stop future access through this installation.

Contact

For privacy questions, contact the operator of Basketball Fever AI Clipper through the contact details provided with the application.
